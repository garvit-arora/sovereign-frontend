The problem

Optimization shouldn't mean a rented black box.

Refineries, power grids and logistics teams depend on mathematical optimization — but most either rent cloud solvers or rely on foreign licenses they can't inspect.

Sovereign answers SIH 26119 with a native C++ core, a web workspace, and a connector that runs on hardware you already own. The coordinator routes; the solve stays local.

Solver core

Native C++ algorithms

Benchmark solvers compare results — they are not the production engine.

LP

Linear programming

Revised simplex + primal-dual interior-point with Mehrotra-style corrector and presolve.

MILP

Mixed-integer LP

Branch-and-bound / branch-and-cut with LP relaxations, cuts, heuristics and branching.

QP

Convex QP

Continuous quadratic optimization with numerical and solution checks.

GPU

CUDA path

Sparse matrix ops on GPU where applicable; remaining steps stay on CPU.

MIQP, NLP and MINLP are roadmap items per the problem statement.
